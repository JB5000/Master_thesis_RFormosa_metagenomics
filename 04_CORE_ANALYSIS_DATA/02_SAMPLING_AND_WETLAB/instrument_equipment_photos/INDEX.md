# Instrument & equipment photos (evidence of the hardware/software used)

**FOR CHATGPT:** These 9 `.jpeg` files are **photographic evidence of the actual laboratory
instruments and software** used in this thesis (taken by the student at CCMAR, University of
Algarve, 2026-07-02). **You can open and read them.** They document equipment identity, serial
numbers and software versions for Methods 2.2–2.4. They complement the protocol photos in
`../experimental_protocol_photos/`.

> Note: some photos show **shared-equipment usage logs** (CCMAR "Registo de Utilização de
> Equipamentos"). Those logs contain entries from **other lab users** (unrelated names, research
> groups and sample types) — only the **equipment make/model/serial** at the top of each log is
> relevant here. Other users' log rows are outside this inventory.

## Equipment provenance summary (for Methods)
- **Sequencing:** Oxford Nanopore **PromethION 2 Solo** (PRO‑SEQ002), unit **P2S‑02226**, running **MinKNOW 26.01.15**.
- **DNA quantification:** Thermo **NanoDrop One^C** (SN AZY1810751).
- **Bead‑beating / homogenization:** **Retsch MM400** (SN 121730123G), 30 Hz.
- **Thermal cycling:** **Applied Biosystems 2720**.
- **Centrifugation:** **Eppendorf 5804** (plate rotor) for pooling; microcentrifuge at 13 200 RPM for the extraction spins (protocol photos).
- **Facility:** CCMAR, University of Algarve, Edifício 7, Laboratório 2.86 (equipment responsible: Marta Valente Bernardo).
